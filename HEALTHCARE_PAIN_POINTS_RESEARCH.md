## 1. PRIMARY CARE & RURAL HEALTHCARE SETTINGS

### What Takes Too Long

| Task | Time Consumed | Source |
|------|---------------|--------|
| EHR documentation per 8hr patient schedule | **5.8 hours** (72.5% of workday) | AMA 2024 study of 200,081 physicians |
| EHR time per 15 min patient visit | **9 minutes** charting notes | Tebra 2025 research |
| After-hours "pajama time" on EHR | **2.7 hours/day** for primary care | AMA 2024 |
| Inbox management per 8hr shift | **1.1 hours** (primary care: 50% more) | AMA-funded study 2022 |
| Total EHR time per day (all tasks) | **4.5 hours** | Medical Economics, Dec 2025 |

**Key finding:** Primary care physicians spend nearly **2 hours on EHR tasks per hour of direct patient care.** The US EHR burden (4.5 hrs/day) is **4.5x higher** than other countries (~1 hr/day).

### Most Common Errors

- **Missed abnormal lab results:** 6.8%--62% of lab results not followed up; 1%--35.7% of radiology results not followed up (systematic review, PMC)
- **Diagnostic errors in primary care:** Internal medicine and family medicine account for **41% of diagnostic error events and 45% of malpractice indemnity paid** (Coverys 2025 report, 6,009 closed events 2020--2024)
- **Missed cancer diagnoses:** Nearly **45% of office-based diagnostic error events** alleged missed cancers (prostate, lung, breast, colorectal most common)
- **EHR alert override rate:** Nearly **75%** of drug alerts are overridden by physicians due to alert fatigue

### Where Doctors Feel Overwhelmed

- **49% of physicians** reported burnout in 2024 (Medscape); **48%** in March 2025
- **Documentation and charting** is the #1 cited burnout contributor (16% of providers in Tebra survey)
- **59% of primary care physicians** believe EHRs need a complete overhaul
- **50% of PCPs** believe EHRs impair clinical effectiveness
- **70% of physicians** say work-related stress hinders quality of life (MDVIP/Ipsos Jan 2025)
- **22.5% of physicians** spend >8 hours on EHR outside normal work hours (up from 20.9% in 2023)

### Physician Quotes

> "The time spent in documentation, getting meds approved, having to fight insurances, and not getting reimbursed fairly." -- Cardiologist respondent, Tebra 2025

> "Listen to physicians and fulfill their needs. The lack of accountability from leadership is astounding... Fix leadership and support physicians adequately." -- KLAS Research 2024

> "Take action when staff members speak up about problems in the office. I often think about leaving because there is no action and I feel as if my voice doesn't matter." -- Clinician, KLAS 2024

### Financial Cost of Burnout

- Physician burnout costs the US healthcare system approximately **$5.6 billion annually**
- **100,000 physicians** estimated to leave the workforce in 2024
- **At least 1 physician per day** in the US dies by suicide (~400/year)
- Over one-quarter of practices had at least one physician retire or leave due to burnout (MGMA poll)

### Wait Times

- **UK:** Every radiology unit surveyed missed waiting-time targets in 2024
- **Global average:** Patient imaging delays of **2+ weeks** are common
- **Each month of delayed cancer diagnosis** increases mortality risk by approximately **10%**
- **AI triage** cut report turnaround times by up to **43.7%** for stroke/PE cases
- For pneumothorax on chest X-ray, AI reduced turnaround from **80.1 min to 35.6 min** (55% reduction)

### Financial Burden

- NHS spent **>$560 million** trying to address imaging demand in 2024
- **$226 million** on teleradiology outsourcing alone
- By 2028, UK outsourcing projected to exceed **$450 million/year** (financially unsustainable)
- Projected radiologist shortage by 2034: **10,300--35,600** (AAMC)

## 3. LAB REPORT INTERPRETATION: THE "MISSED RESULTS" PROBLEM

### How Often Are Abnormal Results Missed

| Metric | Rate | Source |
|--------|------|--------|
| Lab results not followed up | **6.8%--62%** | Systematic review (PMC) |
| Radiology results not followed up | **1.0%--35.7%** | Systematic review (PMC) |
| Hospital inpatients with missed follow-up | **1.03%--22.9%** of inpatients | Systematic review |
| Missed follow-up per test type | **20.0%--61.9%** | Systematic review |
| Lab results that are erroneous | ~**1%** | PMC estimate |
| Lab errors leading to patient harm | **5%--10%** of lab errors | PMC estimate |

### Real-World Impact

- A typical **university hospital performing 2 million tests/year** would have approximately **1,000 lab-related adverse events per year**
- **1 in 3 clinicians** reported missing abnormal test results due to inbox overload
- VA staff unanimously acknowledged **no backup system** for tracking EHR notifications for abnormal results
- Physicians receive **70--75 new inbox notifications per day** on average
- Nearly **70% of diagnostic errors** occurred during the testing process (ordering, collecting, processing, communicating results) -- ECRI 2024 analysis

### Contributing Factors

- **Poor signal-to-noise ratio** in EHR notifications
- **Lack of clear ownership** -- unclear who is responsible for follow-up
- **No compelling visualization** to prioritize notifications by urgency
- Key lab results often **present in the EHR but buried** in unread alerts
- External lab results **lack integration**, leading to missing or delayed results

## 7. TRIAGE AND SCREENING BOTTLENECKS

### Emergency Department Volume

- US ED visits in 2024: **139.8 million** (42.7 per 100 people)
- **90%+** of EDs report routine crowding
- ED volumes projected to grow **5%** over next decade
- Emergent visits for 65+ population expected to grow **28%** in next decade
- 75-84 age cohort: **45% increase** projected
- Behavioral health ED visits: **12% growth** projected over 10 years (with longer length of stay)

### Key Bottlenecks

1. **Doctor-to-Decision Time:** Most significant bottleneck (2025 Saudi Arabia study)
2. **Triage-to-Doctor Time:** Second most significant bottleneck
3. **Inpatient boarding:** Critically ill patients wait too long for beds
4. **Low-acuity flooding:** Patients lacking primary/urgent care access use ED instead

### Impact of Triage Inaccuracy (JAMA Network Open 2025, 5,929 patients)

| Condition | CT Order Delay (undertriage) | Medication Order Delay |
|-----------|------------------------------|----------------------|
| Subarachnoid hemorrhage | **+2.4 minutes** | **+17.6 minutes** |
| Aortic dissection | **+8.9 minutes** | **+33.3 minutes** |

### Dual Bottleneck Problem
Hospitals face simultaneous challenges: critical patients waiting for inpatient beds upstream, while low-acuity patients flood in downstream due to primary/urgent care access barriers.

## INDIA-SPECIFIC DATA: THE SCALE OF THE CRISIS

### Doctor Shortage

| Metric | Data | Source |
|--------|------|--------|
| National doctor-patient ratio | **1:1,000** (meets WHO target overall) | NMC 2024 |
| Rural doctor-patient ratio | **1:11,082** | State of Healthcare in Rural India 2024 |
| Urban-to-rural doctor density ratio | **3.8:1** | Multiple sources |
| Specialist shortage at community health centers | **79.9%** (17,551 of 21,964 positions vacant) | Health Dynamics 2022-23 |
| Specialist shortage in Madhya Pradesh | **94%** | Government data |
| Specialist shortage in Gujarat | **88.1%** | Government data |
| Specialist shortage in Tamil Nadu | **85.2%** | Government data |
| PHC doctor vacancies in Chhattisgarh | **71%** | Government data |
| Healthcare worker density (India) | **20.6 per 10,000** (WHO recommends 44.5) | WHO data |

### Access Gaps

- **86% of medical visits** come from rural residents
- Many travel **>100 km** to reach care
- **70--80% of medical costs** paid out-of-pocket
- Only **39%** have a diagnostic facility within commutable distance
- Only **12.2%** have access to subsidized medicines
- Government health expenditure: **1.28% of GDP**
- Rural areas: **16% fewer PHCs**, **50% fewer CHCs** than recommended
- **90%** of rural respondents do not undergo routine health checkups unless recommended
- In Andhra Pradesh, **86% of 80 million people** live >3 km from nearest hospital

### AI Deployment in India (Bright Spots)

- **Qure.ai qXR:** Deployed in 139+ health facilities; identifies **30--40% more incidental TB cases**; deployed in 2,600+ sites across 67 countries
- **Genki CADe:** 98,000 X-rays screened, 14,500 flagged, 606 TB cases diagnosed (6 districts, Tamil Nadu)
- **TB Mukt Bharat Abhiyan** (100-day drive, Dec 2024): Screening coverage increased from 59% to 85%; **12.97 crore people screened**, **7.19 lakh new TB cases** detected
- **Maha Kumbh Mela 2025:** First-ever AI deployment for TB surveillance at a mass gathering (~50 crore attendees)
- **KMC Manipal Hospital:** AI-enabled CT workflows serve **20--30 more patients daily**
- India's TB burden: **27% of all global TB cases** (2.82 million estimated cases)
